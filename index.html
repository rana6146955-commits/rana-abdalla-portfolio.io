About Me

For me, the interesting part starts when the answer isn’t obvious.

Before I got into data analysis, I already knew I liked figuring things out, especially when something felt unclear or didn’t make sense right away. I enjoyed looking at different pieces, comparing them, and trying to understand how they connected.

Data analysis gave that way of thinking a direction. Now, instead of just being curious about why something happens, I can work through the information, test different possibilities, and turn what looks confusing at first into something much clearer.

I’m still building my experience, but I like how every project gives me a new problem to work through and a better way to think about the next one.
